SAMPLE QUESTIONS AND EXPECTED RESPONSES
=======================================

These prompts cover all four routes used by the Streamlit demo. LLM wording may
vary, but the route, evidence, citations, trend values, and fallback behavior
below should remain consistent.


LITERATURE
----------

Expected response:
  Route: literature
  The answer should summarize relevant papers on low-resource machine
  translation. The Citations panel should contain clickable arXiv links for
  verified relevant papers such as:
    - arXiv:2005.05507
    - arXiv:2009.09359
    - arXiv:2011.05284
    - arXiv:2103.13272
    - arXiv:2104.00290
  No trend chart is expected.
